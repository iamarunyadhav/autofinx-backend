# Risk policy

Nexus has no order, wallet transfer, tuning, deployment, merge or risk-override tool. Those permissions remain false even when higher autonomy values are configured.
Never bypass kill switches, drawdown/exposure limits, stale-price guards, exchange reconciliation or emergency exits.
Approval means approval of a research proposal/build package, not authority to place trades or deploy changes.
An LLM cannot award validation level A. Its references must resolve to supplied evidence IDs; references do not prove the claims themselves.
